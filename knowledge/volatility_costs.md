# Volatility and costs

## Volatility regimes
ATR as a percent of price clusters: quiet periods follow quiet periods. Breakouts from low-volatility compression tend to travel; breakouts during already high volatility tend to fail. A volatility condition is a cheap way to split one rule into the regime where it works and the regime where it does not.

## Volume
Unusually high volume, a z-score above two, marks capitulation or initiation. It confirms breakouts and marks washouts, but as a standalone signal it has no direction.

## Costs and turnover
Each round trip costs about fourteen basis points here. A rule that trades two hundred times a year needs twenty-eight percent of gross return just to break even. Holding periods under six bars rarely survive costs on 4-hour data; fewer, longer trades with the same gross edge score much higher.
